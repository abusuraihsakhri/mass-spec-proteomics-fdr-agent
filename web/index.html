Method and limitations. Target–decoy competition with a +1 decoy correction (TDC+): estimated FDR = min(1, (decoy wins + 1) / target wins) at each score cutoff. Equal scores are grouped; q-values are reverse cumulative minima. Accept only target winners with q ≤ α. Assumes properly constructed, comparable target/decoy searches and one pre-competed winner per spectrum. No search, peptide/protein-level inference, or experimental validation is performed. This is a research utility, not a clinical decision system.
